## Purpose

Preserve reviewed public derivatives of canonical Study OS learning episodes around LeetCode Two Sum where externally authored representation choices created avoidable learning friction for Subject 001.

## What this adds

- `datasets/learner-episodes/2026-09-03-two-sum-representation-translation.json`
  - records control-flow/index-to-value representation translation overhead;
  - preserves the learner's independently proposed `enumerate(...)` alternative;
  - records representation normalization as a prospective intervention hypothesis.
- `datasets/learner-episodes/2026-09-03-two-sum-variable-name-interference.json`
  - records `variable_name_semantic_interference` as an observed subject-level failure signature;
  - preserves the direct confusion caused by `seen`, its prior set-related meaning for the learner, the unsuccessful descriptive rename, and the clearer `box` representation;
  - records non-intuitive identifiers as a teacher/system quality failure when they add avoidable decoding cost without preserving target difficulty;
  - proposes reversible identifier normalization followed by later source-name reintroduction for code-reading robustness.
- `datasets/README.md`
  - defines `learner-episodes/` as reviewed public-safe derivatives of canonical Study OS sessions that do not yet meet Golden Learning Trajectory criteria;
  - preserves the distinction between canonical session origin and durable runtime persistence.

## Evidence interpretation

Supported at subject level:
- externally authored control-flow representation became an immediate learning bottleneck;
- variable naming itself became an avoidable semantic barrier even though renaming did not change program behavior;
- the learner independently proposed a clearer explicit index/value representation;
- the learner explicitly identified `seen` as conflicting with a prior set-related interpretation;
- a neutral concrete identifier was reported as clearer and was followed by a correct dictionary lookup response;
- these episodes support prospectively testing representation and identifier normalization.

The second episode includes a conservative note that the tutor also simplified the surrounding example, so the exact magnitude attributable to naming alone is not quantified. This does not downgrade the direct learner evidence that the identifier itself was a barrier.

Not claimed:
- durable Two Sum or dictionary mastery;
- universal superiority of `enumerate` or `box`;
- a universal naming rule;
- causal long-term learning improvement;
- transfer or retention success.

## Privacy / third-party boundary

No raw transcript is committed. No verbatim LeetCode solution is committed. Public records store reviewed evidence statements, metadata, learner-generated fragments, and representation features only.

## Runtime boundary

The episodes originated in canonical Study OS learning sessions. Reported HTTP 502 failures are tracked separately as failed/unverified durable structured writes; they do not make the sessions noncanonical.

No runtime, application-contract, persistence, learner-state, or adaptive-authority change is introduced by this PR.
