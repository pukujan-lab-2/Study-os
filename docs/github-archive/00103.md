# Issue #103: SOS-0006 Visual identity: grown-up anime style with sticker/mascot set via CGM (with provenance)

- State: open
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-09-24

Parent: #82 (web app epic) · Related: #101 (SOS-0005 lesson player redesign) · Dependencies: none

## Goal
Give Study OS a visual identity: an anime-style look with cute sticker-style illustrations that still feels grown-up. A clean adult study UI with tasteful mascot/sticker accents, not a children's app.

## Scope (updated with Alex's decisions, 2026-09-24)
- Use the pinned CGM helper (`Pukujan/content-generation-modules`, pinned in `.content-system/system-version.json`) for brand, visual style and the image pipeline. CGM palette: night ink #111B4D, violet #8F7CFF, cyan #63D9FF, coral #FF8A70, cream #F4F1FF, mint #94E3CB, gold #FFD28A, with blue-violet evening light. Follow CGM `docs/IMAGE_GUIDE.md` (a hand-authored SVG does not replace an image) and `modules/image-generation/SKILL.md` (prompt recipe, candidate review, asset record).
- **Canonical, persistent characters** go in the character bible `.content-system/characters/`: a grown-up anime **Learner** (human) and a robot **Helper**. The bible holds the written spec, turnaround and expression-sheet references from a real image model, and fixed prompt blocks plus model and settings. All future art is based on these references.
- **General art** (onboarding, lane cards, empty states, hero, lesson illustrations) uses the mature anime style.
- **In-app mascot roles:**
  - The **PET / main mascot is the chibi Learner (human).** Tapping it opens the companion panel. It talks (talking loop, TTS, and a speech bubble with the live transcript), reacts to answers (celebrate / encourage) and idles otherwise.
  - The **chibi robot Helper is a supporting character.** It only appears during idle: after a stretch of idle time it occasionally rolls in, plays with the pet, and leaves. This is rare and non-distracting. It never happens during assessment or while the learner is typing or speaking.
- **Loops** are frame-by-frame image sequences of 4–9 frames on one aligned transparent canvas, packed as WebP sprite sheets (under ~60 KB each), played with CSS `steps()` and paused under prefers-reduced-motion:
  - pet: idle (breathe, blink, small fidgets), wave, thinking, talking, celebrate, encourage
  - pet + robot idle interaction loops, plus a robot enter/exit hover
- **Companion is one screen with one shared state** (see #101 and the design doc §9). The question card always stays visible. Tapping the pet opens a half-height sheet on mobile or a side panel on desktop, holding the transcript, a text box and mic/speaker toggles. Worked examples and easier/harder variants update the card in place, with a tag and a Back control.
- Provenance for every asset goes in `.content-system/asset-manifest.json`.
- Out of scope: marketing site, video.

**Sprites** in `web/public/mascot/*.webp`, transparent, frames aligned on one canvas:
- pet loops: 144x176 frames, 32–35 KB each
- duo loops: 216x144 frames, 55 KB and 62 KB
- `robot-hover`: 120x160 frames, 20 KB

**Robot visit rules**
- Triggers after 90 s or more of idle time, at most once every 10 min, and stays on screen for 6 s or less.
- Never runs during an assessment probe, while the learner is typing or speaking, or while the pet is talking, thinking or reacting.
- Turned off under reduced motion or when the mascot is hidden.

**Pipeline and provenance**
- Model: `cb/gemini-3.1-flash-image` via InferHub. Canonical refs and the CGM `story-loop-square.png` style anchor are attached as image inputs.
- Loops are generated as 3x2 or 3x3 sheets on chroma green, then processed with chroma key, grid split, edge grid-line removal and union-bbox alignment into WebP strips.
- Asset manifest: 16 entries (7 character refs, 9 sprites), each with model, prompt file, prompt sha256, reference hashes, source and final sha256, and review status.
- CGM adapter validator: `VALID`.
- Rejected: an earlier robot turnaround with baked-in text labels, and the old SVG fox, per Alex.

**Cost:** 33 image generations, about **$0.025** in InferHub spend (estimated from token usage at the model's listed price).

**Known limits (draft)**
- The pet's size varies slightly between frames inside a loop.
- The duo loops contain the pet at about 0.77 of its solo scale, so the app renders them at 1.3x to keep her size steady.
- There is no provider seed, so reproducibility relies on the prompt file, its hash and the reference hashes.
